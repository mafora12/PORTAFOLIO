Ariel in 10, 30 & 50 years

Imagining the future of a brand — from mobile apps to a VR experience and a 150 cm product model.

My role
3D modeling, motion graphics, VR interface, prototyping of every interface and stand layout
Team
Sofía Bedoya, Catalina Cervantes, Juanes Arango and me
Timeline
3 months · 2025
Tools
Figma, Blender, Unity, After Effects, Illustrator, Photoshop

The challenge

Imagine what Ariel could be in 10, 30 and 50 years, and present that vision through products, interfaces and a stand.

What I did

We researched as a team. I modeled every object in 3D, laid out the stand, designed the VR interface, made the animations and prototyped all the interfaces.

Key decisions

  • I proposed making the 50-year version a VR experience, and solving several pieces in 3D. The team approved both ideas.

Outcome

A VR experience combined with 3D for Ariel in 50 years, apps for 10 and 30 years, a 150 cm scale model of the product, its packaging at scale, a function infographic, a brand timeline and a stand people tried out.

What I learned

It was the first time I combined both of my degrees in one project. Next time I would refine the interfaces, the VR experience and the textures.
