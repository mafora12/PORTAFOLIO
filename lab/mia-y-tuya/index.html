Mía y tuya

An illustrated book of real experiences with menstruation — one that invites the reader to add her own.

Context
Research project & grade project
Format
Solo
Year
2024
Tools
Illustrator, Photoshop, InDesign

The brief

A book that gathers real women’s experiences with their periods and invites whoever reads it to share theirs too.

What I did

I researched the topic — problem, theoretical framework and hypothesis — collected testimonies and turned them into an illustrated, laid-out book with spaces for the reader to take part.

Why I like it

The whole journey: the stories I heard, the people I shared it with, and how all of it shaped the book.
